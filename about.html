The Gran Trade Story

Two decades of stone expertise in Carlstadt, New Jersey

Founded in 2003 and based in Carlstadt, New Jersey, Gran Trade Inc. has grown into one of the region’s most trusted wholesale distributors of natural and engineered stone, supplying clients across New Jersey, New York, Connecticut, and Pennsylvania.

The team brings more than two decades of hands-on experience and works side-by-side with fabricators to meet what architects, designers, and homeowners ask for. Strong, longstanding supplier relationships across Italy, Brazil, India, Spain, and domestic US quarries allow us to import fine material at the lowest possible cost — and an efficient supply chain keeps our partners stocked day after day.

We do not treat stone as an abstract commodity. Every container arriving at our Carlstadt facility is unbanded, crane-lifted, and inspected slab by slab to ensure resin consistency, thickness calibration, and structural integrity.

How We Operate

A wholesale model built around the fabricator

We supply raw stone slabs and monuments to the trades. Here is how our distribution network powers projects from concept to completion.

01

Direct Quarry Sourcing

We bypass middlemen and contract directly with primary quarries in Carrara, Bahia, Rajasthan, and Alicante. This eliminates markup and gives us first pick of sequential blocks.

02

Deep Consecutive Bundles

We stock full bundles cut sequentially from the same mountain block. Fabricators can bookmatch veins for expansive islands and multi-slab installations without color shifts.

03

Indoor Yard Staging

Our Carlstadt warehouse is equipped with heavy overhead gantry cranes. We pull slabs safely into staging bays so designers and homeowners can examine full bundles under proper lighting.

04

Rapid Tri-State Delivery

With an active A-frame fleet and convenient access to the NJ Turnpike, Route 3, and Route 17, we deliver bundles directly to fabricator shops with minimal lead times.

Operational Standards

Our wholesale trade commitment

Strictly Wholesale

Gran Trade Inc. is a slab importer and distributor. We do not fabricate or install counters. We protect our fabricator partners by never quoting installation prices directly to homeowners.

Homeowners Welcome

Homeowners are invited to walk our yard, inspect full bundles, and tag their favorite slabs. Once tagged, we coordinate bundle holds directly with your fabricator of choice.
