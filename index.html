Metamorphic Quartz Sandstone

Quartzite

Nature's ultimate metamorphic masterpiece. Born from pure quartz sandstone recrystallized under colossal tectonic pressure, quartzite pairs the ethereal crystalline luminescence of fine marble with extraordinary mineral density. Naturally impervious to etching from wine or citrus juices, knife scratches, and daily kitchen wear.

Granite

Pure volcanic fortitude formed by molten magma slowly crystallizing miles beneath the continental crust. Interlocked quartz, feldspar, and mica crystals produce unmatched structural strength, deep chromatic depth, and enduring resilience against heavy culinary prep, outdoor UV exposure, and all-weather cycles.

Engineered Surfacing
93% Pure Quartz Composite

Quartz

Advanced architectural surfacing engineered for contemporary family living and high-traffic commercial environments. Composed of 93% pure crushed natural quartz crystal fused with advanced polymer resins. Zero porosity ensures liquids and oils never penetrate, requiring zero sealing or chemical conditioning over its lifespan.

Formats & Thickness Jumbo Slabs (63" x 128") · 3 cm
Available Textures High Polish Brilliant · Soft Touch Matte (Honed)
Ideal Applications Modern Family Kitchens · Multi-Unit Developments · Hospitality Suites
Popular On The Racks Calacatta Alps · Versilia · Calacatta Gold · Pure Arctic White

Natural Stone
Metamorphic Steatite Talc Rock

Soapstone

An authentic artisanal heritage stone prized for its silky, velvety tactile touch. Naturally non-porous and chemically inert, making it completely impervious to lemon juice, vinegar, red wine, and harsh acids without requiring sealers. Gradually develops an organic, rich charcoal patina when seasoned with food-safe mineral oil.

Available Textures Honed Silky Matte
Ideal Applications Artisanal & Farmhouse Kitchens · Fireplace Hearths · Chemistry Benches
Popular On The Racks Churchill Soapstone · Barroca Black · Mariana Grey

Structural Granite
Certified Structural Granite

Monuments

Certified structural-grade natural granites quarried for multi-generational outdoor permanence. Dense, uniform crystalline matrix withstands severe Northeastern freeze-thaw cycles, heavy precipitation, and atmospheric weathering without surface degradation. Provides high contrast for diamond sandblasting and precision laser engraving.
