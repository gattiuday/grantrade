The Wholesale Advantage

Built for the trade, open to everyone

We work directly with quarries to provide Northeast fabricators, designers, and project managers with unmatched inventory reliability.

01

Acclaimed at the source

Our products come from premier global quarries widely acclaimed for slab clarity. Every ocean container is inspected at our Carlstadt warehouse upon arrival.

02

Fully customizable choice

Pick from 60+ curated natural and engineered stone options — a range tailored to diverse architectural styles, high-traffic commercial spaces, and custom homes.

03

Built to perform

Durable, cleanable, high-grade stone stocked deep in consecutive bundles so your fabricator can match veins and fulfill multi-room project requirements without batch variations.

04

Service you can call

We take pride in our direct customer relations. Real stone specialists answer our phones, know the current rack locations, and coordinate seamless delivery logistics.
