# Gran Trade Inc. — Content Rules & Editorial Guidelines

This document governs the editorial tone, wholesale positioning rules, the strict **"No Quote CTAs" policy**, and the **"Facts to Confirm" verification checklist** for Gran Trade Inc.

---

## 1. Brand Voice & Positioning

### Who Gran Trade Inc. Is
- **Wholesale Natural & Engineered Stone Importer**: Founded in 2003 in Carlstadt, New Jersey (178 Commerce Road).
- **Core Audience**: Stone fabricators, kitchen & bath showrooms, commercial general contractors, interior designers, landscape architects, and monument retailers across the Northeast (NJ, NY, CT, PA).
- **Homeowner Posture**: Welcoming and supportive. Homeowners are encouraged to walk the yard, view full-size slabs, and select consecutive bundles for their projects, while being clearly informed that fabrication and installation pricing is arranged through their chosen stone fabricator.

### Tone Principles
- **Material-Literate & Direct**: Speak accurately about geological properties (e.g. *brecciated marble*, *larvikite feldspar*, *90%+ quartz crystalline density*, *leathered diamond-brush texture*).
- **No Fluff or Hyperbole**: Avoid generic retail marketing phrases like *"The best prices in town!"* or *"Unbeatable deals!"*. Let the physical stone slabs and two decades of wholesale relationships speak for themselves.
- **Original Copy Only**: Every headline, description, and paragraph must be bespoke to Gran Trade Inc. No placeholder lorem ipsum, no boilerplate template text.

## 2. Strict "No Quote CTAs" Policy

### Why Wholesale Distributors Never Quote Installation
Gran Trade Inc. imports and distributes whole, uncut stone slabs. We do **not** cut sink holes, bevel edges, miter waterfalls, or carry slabs into residential kitchens.
Countertop pricing depends almost entirely on fabrication labor, edge profile complexity, seam placement, cutouts, and on-site rigging. If a distributor quotes a generic "square foot price", it creates customer confusion and undermines our professional relationship with certified fabricators.

### Forbidden Phrases & CTAs (Strictly Disallowed)
❌ *"Get a Free Quote"*  
❌ *"Request an Instant Quote"*  
❌ *"Calculate Your Countertop Cost"*  
❌ *"Price Per Square Foot: $XX"*  
❌ *"Buy Now / Add to Cart"*  
❌ *"Free In-Home Estimate"*  

### Permitted & Recommended Wholesale CTAs
✅ *"Come walk the yard"*  
✅ *"Check slab availability"*  
✅ *"Plan a warehouse visit"*  
✅ *"Call the yard: (201) 438-1888"*  
✅ *"Hold a consecutive bundle"*  
✅ *"Bring your fabricator or borrow ours"*  
✅ *"Inquire about slab lot numbers"*  
✅ *"Download slab specifications"*

## 4. Architectural Glossary for Copywriting

- **Bundle**: A sequence of 6 to 10 slabs cut consecutively from the same quarried stone block, sharing identical vein patterns and coloring.
- **Bookmatch**: Two consecutive slabs polished on opposite faces (A and B) that mirror each other when installed side by side.
- **Honed**: A smooth, low-sheen matte finish achieved without the final high-speed buffing step.
- **Leathered**: A tactile, textured finish created by abrasive diamond brushes that enhances natural fissure topography while concealing fingerprints and water spots.
- **Engineered Quartz**: Surfacing composed of approximately 93% crushed natural quartz aggregates bound with polymer resins and color pigments. Non-porous and maintenance-free.
- **Fissure vs. Crack**: A fissure is a naturally occurring mineral vein formed during geological metamorphism and does not compromise slab integrity. A crack is a mechanical fracture resulting from impact or improper transport.
