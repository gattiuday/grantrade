Planning Your Visit

What to expect at the Carlstadt yard

We keep our facility clean, accessible, and organized so you can inspect material safely and efficiently.

01. Walk-in Welcomed

Trade professionals and homeowners are welcome without an appointment Monday through Saturday. Bring cabinet samples, paint swatches, or tile chips to compare directly against natural slabs.

02. Crane-Assisted Staging

Want to see a specific slab tucked behind others in a bundle? Our warehouse crew uses overhead gantry cranes to safely pull slabs forward into dedicated staging areas under direct daylight lamps.

03. Tagging & Bundle Holds

Found the exact slab for your project? We apply physical hold tags with your project name. We coordinate directly with your certified fabricator to arrange delivery.
